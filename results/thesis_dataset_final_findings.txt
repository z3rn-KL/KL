FINAL DATASET-SCALE BENCHMARK FINDINGS
================================================================

Benchmark:
- 108 workloads
- 813 deliveries per algorithm
- workload size 5–16
- 540 algorithm-workload runs

Overall route quality:
- Nearest Neighbor: 62.6803 km, 68.1338 min.
- Clarke-Wright: 58.0539 km, 63.2741 min.
- Q-Learning: 62.2624 km, 67.6854 min.
- SARSA: 62.8589 km, 68.3059 min.
- DQN: 59.8287 km, 65.1267 min.

Relative route improvements:
- Clarke-Wright vs NN: 7.38% distance reduction, 7.13% travel-time reduction.
- DQN vs NN: 4.55% distance reduction, 4.41% travel-time reduction.

Delivery-weighted SLA:
- NN: 99.14% (7 late).
- Clarke-Wright: 99.14% (7 late).
- Q-Learning: 100.00% (0 late).
- SARSA: 99.75% (2 late).
- DQN: 100.00% (0 late).

RL computational trade-off:
- Q-Learning mean training: 2.75 s.
- SARSA mean training: 2.61 s.
- DQN mean training: 191.52 s.
- DQN requires approximately 69.6x the mean training time of Q-Learning.

Interpretation:
- Clarke-Wright remains the strongest method for pure distance/time minimization.
- DQN is the strongest RL method in route quality and reaches 100% delivery-weighted on-time rate.
- Q-Learning also reaches 100% on-time rate with much lower training cost.
- SARSA reduces late deliveries relative to the heuristic baselines but is weaker than DQN overall.
- Reward per delivery should be used instead of raw episode reward when comparing different workload sizes.
- Results for N=14, 15 and 16 are case evidence only because each size contains one workload.
